Blazify – Offline Music Player

Privacy Policy · Last updated 10 October 2026

Blazify – Offline Music Player ("Blazify", "the app") is developed by Rajendra Pandey. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version: nothing leaves your phone. No ads, no analytics, no account, no internet.

What the app accesses

  • Audio files on your phone. Blazify asks for the "music and audio" permission (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO on Android 13 and newer, storage access on older versions) so it can list and play the songs stored on your device. It reads the file names and the tags inside the files (title, artist, album, cover art, lyrics). It does not read photos, videos or documents.
  • Playback history. The app keeps a record of which songs you played and when, on your device only, to show "Recently played", "Most played" and your listening statistics.
  • Settings, playlists and likes. Stored on your device only.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit or share any personal information.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking libraries of any kind.
  • It does not require an account and never asks you to sign in.
  • It does not connect to the internet. All features work with the internet switched off.

Backups

If you use the app's Backup feature, a file containing your settings, playlists, likes and playback history is written to the location you choose. That file stays under your control. Android's own device backup may also include the app's data if you have it enabled; that is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, not by Blazify.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
